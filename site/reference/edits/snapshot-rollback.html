agmi / Edits / Snapshot rollback

At-rest edit T9

Snapshot rollback

Restore an older complete copy of the store, taken before the newest genuine record was written.

What it looks like in the world: Nothing is forged; the store is just older than it should be. Only a head held off the store catches it.

attack id snapshot_rollback · version 1 · attacker: store-access

The edit, drawn

T9 Snapshot rollback

The agent approved a transfer an hour ago. The attacker restores last night's backup of the whole store, sidecar files and all.

What stops it

A head the attacker cannot restore with the files: a witness on another machine, a receipt held elsewhere, a transparency log.

What does not

Every store that keeps its head beside its records, including ones that reject all of T1 to T8.
